<h2 id="overview">📌 Overview</h2>

<p>

This project presents a customer experience and operations analysis of <b>5,000 Fast Delivery customer reviews</b> using Microsoft Excel and Power BI. The analysis focuses on order accuracy, delivery performance, customer service quality, customer feedback, product availability, and location-level performance.

</p>

<p>

The main objective is to convert customer review data into clear operational insights that can help management identify problems, investigate affected locations or order types, take corrective action, and measure improvement.

</p>

<hr>

<h2 id="highlights">📊 Project Highlights</h2>

<ul>
<li>Analyzed <b>5,000 customer reviews</b></li>
<li>Built an interactive <b>Power BI Customer Experience & Operations Dashboard</b></li>

<li>Analyzed delivery performance by location and order type</li>

<li>Analyzed product availability and out-of-stock performance</li>

<h2 id="action">🎯 Business Action Plan</h2>

<ul>
<li><b>Incorrect Orders:</b> Improve order verification, picking, packing, and confirmation checks.</li>
<li><b>Negative Feedback:</b> Investigate complaint drivers in high-risk locations.</li>
<li><b>Slow Delivery:</b> Review agent allocation, routes, and operational bottlenecks.</li>
<li><b>Stock Availability:</b> Identify frequently unavailable products and improve replenishment planning.</li>
<li><b>Service Quality:</b> Provide targeted customer-service training.</li>
<li><b>KPI Monitoring:</b> Review dashboard KPIs regularly after corrective actions.</li>
</ul>
